4. Find out the benefit and test the price against it. Ask the client four questions before you settle the figure.

  • What problem must be solved now?
  • What happens if nothing is done?
  • Which pieces of work would make the greatest difference?
  • What certainty or control do you need?

The amount at stake is context. It supplies no formula. Where value cannot sensibly be put in figures, price the agreed service from scope and cost and use the client’s aims as a check.

7. Check fairness before you send it

Cost is where the price starts. It is not a legal minimum and not a licence. The fee must be fair and reasonable under Canon IV(f) in the actual circumstances, and the client may have the bill taxed under section 22 of the Legal Profession Act.

Staging does not promise that a whole contested case can be conducted for a fixed amount. A stage fee paid in advance is client money until the work is done and billed.
