Tool H

Where does this money go?

A payment arrives. Before it is banked, decide what it is. The word “retainer” on the letter does not settle that. What the money is for does. Answer two questions and see how the paper reads the Legal Profession (Accounts and Records) Regulations, 1999.

Taking money out again

  • Bill, then transfer. Money may be drawn from trust towards fees supported by a delivered interim or final bill. reg 4(5)(i)(c)
  • How it leaves. By a cheque to the attorney or a transfer to the attorney’s own account that is not a clients’ trust account. reg 4(6)
  • Never overdraw a client. Nothing may be paid for a client beyond what is held for that client, and the account as a whole must always meet every obligation. regs 4(5)(ii), 4(9)
  • No contracting out. An agreement or retainer that excludes the Regulations is void. reg 2(3)
  • Reconcile and keep. Reconcile client ledger balances with the bank at least once every five weeks for each calendar month and keep the records for at least nine years. regs 5, 7(2)

What this is not. This is the paper’s summary of the Regulations as amended in 2017, 2019 and 2021, arranged as questions. It is not legal advice and it cannot tell you whether a particular receipt or transfer is lawful. Read the Regulations themselves, which are linked from Paper and sources, and take advice or ask the General Legal Council where you are unsure.

Failure to comply with the Regulations is professional misconduct under regulation 17. Financial pressure is no defence. An advance for work not yet done is not office cash.
