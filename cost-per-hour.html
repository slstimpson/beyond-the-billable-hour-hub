What you keep of an hour

Cost is not price. This figure tells you what an hour of fee-earning work costs your practice. It does not establish what you may charge. A fee must be fair and reasonable under Canon IV(f), judged on the work, the responsibility, the difficulty, the urgency and the other factors there, in the circumstances of the actual matter.

If you billed more hours

Fixed costs do not move when you record another hour, so each additional hour carries a smaller share of them. This is the most useful screen for a sole practitioner deciding whether the answer is a higher rate or a fuller diary.

What to do with this figure

  • Carry it into one matter, four prices as the allocated cost of an hour.
  • Put it on your monthly scorecard as measure one and recalculate it as your costs change.
  • Compare it with what you actually collect for an hour. If the second is lower than the first, the work is being done below its cost.
