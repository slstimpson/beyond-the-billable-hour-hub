Month by month

Client money is not in this model and must never be in your cash plan. Money held in the clients' trust account belongs to the client. An advance for work not yet done is client money, whatever the engagement calls it, and it may be transferred only against fees properly earned and billed, or as the law otherwise permits. The Legal Profession (Accounts and Records) Regulations, 1999, as amended, govern this. Nothing here can tell you whether a particular transfer is lawful.

Three things shorten the wait. Bill promptly, because nothing can be collected before it is billed. Agree the payment dates when you agree the work, not when the bill is late. Give one named person responsibility for following up.

Where a client is suitable for it, a payment plan by standing order across the life of the matter turns an uncertain lump into predictable receipts. Any suspension of work for non-payment must comply with Canon IV(n) and (o), your engagement and the court's procedure.
