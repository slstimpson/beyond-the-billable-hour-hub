An illustration of a calculation, not a pay scheme. Neither scheme is a sufficient measure of quality, fairness or ethical conduct. Any variable award should depend on satisfactory work, proper supervision and compliance with professional obligations. The size of any bonus pool must first be tested against collected income, staff costs, tax, reserves and working capital.

The Canons set limits. Canon IV(a) forbids partnership or fee sharing concerning the practice of law with non-qualified persons or bodies. A bonus for staff who are not attorneys, calculated as a share of the fees on particular matters, could be characterised as fee sharing. The safer course is salary and a performance bonus assessed against the person’s own duties. Canon IV(g) forbids a fee or reward for merely introducing a client.
