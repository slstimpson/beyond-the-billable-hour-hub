Before you use these tools

Model  These are illustrative financial estimates for educational use. They are not legal, accounting or tax advice, and they do not establish what any fee should be.

Law  A fee that looks attractive in a model must still be fair and reasonable under Canon IV(f) of the Canons of Professional Ethics, judged in the actual circumstances of the matter.

Law  Money held in the clients' trust account is not office cash. An advance for work not yet done is client money, whatever it is called. None of these tools treats it as available cash, and none of them can tell you whether a particular transfer is lawful.

Model  No Jamaican benchmarks are offered here, because none have been verified. Compare your results with your own history, not with a figure on this page.
