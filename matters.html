Tool F

Ten past matters

The first action in the paper’s 30-day plan. Take up to ten completed matters of one recurring type and set what each one actually collected against what it actually cost to deliver. Use the fee received, not the fee recorded or billed.

Matter by matter

What this does and does not show. A matter collected below its cost is a prompt to look at scope, staffing, write-offs and the price. It is not proof that the fee was wrong. A practice may knowingly reduce a fee for access to justice or for a relationship. It should know the cost when it does.

Firm-wide averages hide matters that lose money, which is why the paper asks for this matter by matter. No Jamaican benchmark exists for any of these figures. Compare them with your own.

What to do next

  • If this type of work can be scoped reliably, price one stage of it from its cost.
  • Put the overall margin on your monthly scorecard as matter contribution margin.
  • Repeat the exercise for the same type of work in six months and compare.
