Three-minute check

Five questions about how the practice is run, not about your clients. You will get three priorities worth attending to and somewhere to start. Nothing you choose leaves this browser.

What this is not. Five self-reported answers identify possible management priorities. This is not a financial audit, it produces no score, and it says nothing about whether your practice is financially sound. Only your own figures can tell you that, which is what the calculators are for.
